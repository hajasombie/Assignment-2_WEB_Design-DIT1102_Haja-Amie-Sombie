Who We Are

Student Accommodation Website (SAW), is a dedicated online portal established by local students to make finding off-campus accommodation easier and safer. We connect university students across Freetown—including Limkokwing, FBC, IPAM, and Njala—with example landlords and private hostels. Our platform aims to eliminate the hassle of physical searching and protects students from rental scams, offering a transparent and straightforward way to find a place to stay.
